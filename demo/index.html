A few little questions.

How do I place presents on my website?

Mark an element with data-grinch-gift. The package draws an illustrated present on its upper edge. You can also pass a selector or a list of elements.

Does he keep up when the page moves?

He uses document coordinates. Present anchors follow scrolling, moving elements and ordinary layout changes, including while paused. Overflow and native top-layer dialogs still apply.

What about reduced motion and touch?

Reduced-motion visitors get a still character. Touch scrolling stays native, and the overlay lets normal clicks pass through. Optional visitor pause controls are available.

Can I use him with Winter?

Yes. The packages run independently. Add data-grinch-gift to your snow-covered surfaces and let both characters share the scene.
