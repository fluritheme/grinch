EVEN MISCHIEF HAS MANNERS.

A good little guest.

Will he steal my actual content?

Just the illustrated presents. Your buttons, images and text stay exactly where you put them. The decorative Canvas lets normal clicks and scrolling through.

Does he work on phones?

Yes. The resident scales for smaller screens. A tap gets his attention; your page scrolls naturally. With reduced motion enabled, he stays still and does not start heists.

What happens when his heart grows?

All the presents fly back to their elements. He becomes friendly and waves instead of planning the next heist. Start a new heist or reset the scene to bring back the mischief.

Can he share a website with Winter?

Yes. The themes run independently. Snow on the ledges, a little yeti with other plans, and one suspiciously full sack. You can control or pause each resident separately.
